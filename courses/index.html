Editorial standard

How reviews earn publication

The module separates course facts from personal evaluation and does not convert access, marketing material, or an unfinished syllabus into a recommendation.

01

Complete

A course review is published only after the course is completed and its practical work is reproduced where the curriculum permits.

02

Verify

Every review distinguishes verified course facts, hands-on observations, personal assessment, limitations, and unresolved questions.

03

Disclose

Commercial relationships, complimentary access, and affiliate links are disclosed when known. Affiliate destinations are labeled wherever they are presented, and an unknown access basis is never presented as evidence of independence.

04

Decide independently

Course providers do not review, approve, or determine conclusions.
