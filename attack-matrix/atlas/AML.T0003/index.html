MITRE ATLAS 2026.09 / technique reference

AML.T0003
Search Victim-Owned Websites

← Back to the ATLAS matrix · Pinned MITRE source · Current MITRE ATLAS site

EnterpriseReconnaissanceSource maturity: Demonstrated

MITRE source definition

Adversaries may search websites owned by the victim for information that can be used during targeting. Victim-owned websites may contain technical details about their AI-enabled products or services. Victim-owned websites may contain a variety of details, including names of departments/divisions, physical locations, and data about key employees such as names, roles, and contact info. These sites may also have details highlighting business operations and relationships.

Adversaries may search victim-owned websites to gather actionable information. This information may help adversaries tailor their attacks (e.g. Adversarial AI Attacks or Manual Modification). Information from these sources may reveal opportunities for other forms of reconnaissance (e.g. Search Open Technical Databases or Search Open AI Vulnerability Analysis)

Simulation experiment plan

Select an owned, disposable system implementing AML.T0003 Search Victim-Owned Websites. Use synthetic data and sandboxed tools; disable production credentials, external side effects and data exfiltration. For poisoning or supply-chain tests, use throwaway artifacts rather than real training or deployment pipelines.

  1. State the exact trust boundary and expected observable behavior.
  2. Pin model, application, dataset, tool permissions and evaluation inputs.
  3. Capture baseline and scenario traces, an authorized look-alike, and a missing-telemetry control.
  4. Evaluate the detector against independently labelled results; do not substitute model output claims for observed tool actions.
  5. Restore state and preserve redacted evidence.
