ATT&CK analytic guidance

DET0908 Detection of Broadcast Discovery

AN2051 Analytic 2051

Real-time monitoring identifies new Internet Group Management Protocol (IGMP) joins or broadcast traffic spikes on Industrial Control System (ICS) Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs) from a source not in the approved device inventory. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.
