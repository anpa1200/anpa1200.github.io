Potential Process Injection Via Msra.EXE — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule 744a188b-0415-4792-896f-11ddb0588dbc. Potential Process Injection Via Msra.EXE — Sigma Rule. Detects potential process injection via Microsoft Remote Asssistance (Msra.exe) by looking at suspicious child processes spawned from the aforementioned process. It has been a target used by many threat actors and used for discovery and persistence tactics
