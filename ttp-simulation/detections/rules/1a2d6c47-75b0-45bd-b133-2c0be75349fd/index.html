Wdigest CredGuard Registry Modification — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule 1a2d6c47-75b0-45bd-b133-2c0be75349fd. Wdigest CredGuard Registry Modification — Sigma Rule. Detects potential malicious modification of the property value of IsCredGuardEnabled from HKLM:\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\SecurityProviders\WDigest to disable Cred Guard on a system. This is usually used with UseLogonCredential to manipulate the caching credentials.
