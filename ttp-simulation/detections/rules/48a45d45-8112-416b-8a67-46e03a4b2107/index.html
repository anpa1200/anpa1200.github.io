Remove Account From Domain Admin Group — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule 48a45d45-8112-416b-8a67-46e03a4b2107. Remove Account From Domain Admin Group — Sigma Rule. Adversaries may interrupt availability of system and network resources by inhibiting access to accounts utilized by legitimate users. Accounts may be deleted, locked, or manipulated (ex: changed credentials) to remove access to accounts.
