LSASS Dump Keyword In CommandLine — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule ffa6861c-4461-4f59-8a41-578c39f3f23e. LSASS Dump Keyword In CommandLine — Sigma Rule. Detects the presence of the keywords "lsass" and ".dmp" in the commandline, which could indicate a potential attempt to dump or create a dump of the lsass process.
