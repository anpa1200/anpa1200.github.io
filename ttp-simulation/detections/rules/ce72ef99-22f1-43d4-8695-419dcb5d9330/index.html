Suspicious Windows Service Tampering — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule ce72ef99-22f1-43d4-8695-419dcb5d9330. Suspicious Windows Service Tampering — Sigma Rule. Detects the usage of binaries such as 'net', 'sc' or 'powershell' in order to stop, pause, disable or delete critical or important Windows services such as AV, Backup, etc. As seen being used in some ransomware scripts
