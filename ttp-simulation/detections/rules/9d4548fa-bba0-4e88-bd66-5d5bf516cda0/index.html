Masquerading as Linux Crond Process — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule 9d4548fa-bba0-4e88-bd66-5d5bf516cda0. Masquerading as Linux Crond Process — Sigma Rule. Masquerading occurs when the name or location of an executable, legitimate or malicious, is manipulated or abused for the sake of evading defenses and observation. Several different variations of this technique have been observed.
