Notepad++ Updater DNS Query to Uncommon Domains — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule 2074e137-1b73-4e2d-88ba-5a3407dbdce0. Notepad++ Updater DNS Query to Uncommon Domains — Sigma Rule. Detects when the Notepad++ updater (gup.exe) makes DNS queries to domains that are not part of the known legitimate update infrastructure. This could indicate potential exploitation of the updater mechanism or suspicious network activity that warrants further investigation.
