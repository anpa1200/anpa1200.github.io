Fsutil Suspicious Invocation — Sigma Rule

Sigma rule add64136-62e5-48ea-807e-88638d02df1e. Fsutil Suspicious Invocation — Sigma Rule. Detects suspicious parameters of fsutil (deleting USN journal, configuring it with small size, etc). Might be used by ransomwares during the attack (seen by NotPetya and others).
