T1638 Adversary-in-the-Middle — Attack Simulation

Adversaries may attempt to position themselves between two or more networked devices to support follow-on behaviors such as Transmitted Data Manipulation or Endpoint Denial of Service. Adversary-in-the-Middle can be achieved through several mechanisms.
